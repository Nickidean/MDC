The Mindful Digital Collective × DP World Tour

An invitation to bring golf to MDC Camps this August, and get children into the game early.

Hey, I'm Nick. My brother-in-law Rob works for the DP World Tour, and I thought it was worth a shot: could we create a small but meaningful partnership, one that puts golf in front of hundreds of children who might otherwise never pick up a club? Here's the idea.

Who we are

The Mindful Digital Collective is a Community Interest Company delivering digital literacy and life skills workshops in primary schools across Dorset, Surrey and beyond. Our aim is simple: confident, thoughtful young people who can navigate the modern world. A big part of that is getting children off screens, outdoors, and trying sports earlier, because the sports you try as a child become the sports you love for life.

This summer, that idea becomes 🏕️ MDC Camps.

The person behind it

MDC was founded by Nick Peasant, a product designer with 20 years in the tech industry. It started in early 2025, when his Year 6 daughter asked for a phone, and he realised that even after a career spent building technology, he didn't know the right answer. That uncertainty became an after-school club at her school, and grew into everything MDC does today: sessions built around real-life scenarios and open discussion, never lectures. The camp takes that same spirit outdoors.

The opportunity: MDC Camps, 17 to 28 August

🏕️ MDC Camps runs at Litton Lakes in Dorset this August. It's two weeks of activity, discussion and inspiration for primary-age children, including funded places through Dorset Council's Holiday Activities and Food programme, so the camp reaches families who need it most.

Sport runs right through it. Children swim and paddleboard on the lake, an LTA tennis coach runs sessions, and our guest lineup already includes Paralympians Taylor Gough and Kylie Grimes MBE. The whole point is giving children their first taste of as many sports as possible, in a place where trying something new feels safe and fun.

Golf should be one of those sports. For most of these children, a summer camp session would be the first time they ever hold a club. That first swing is where every golfer starts.

Why the DP World Tour

Growing the game from the grassroots up is already your mission. The European Tour group helped create GolfSixes with the Golf Foundation, a team format built to get children into golf young, and it launched the G4D Tour to open the game up for golfers with disabilities. Every part of that says the same thing: golf is for everyone, and it starts early.

We're that story on your home turf: a grassroots project in Dorset, with Paralympians already on our guest list, ready to put golf in front of children who wouldn't otherwise find it.

What we're asking

In order of effort. Any of these would be brilliant:

  1. Donate some kit. Junior clubs, balls, caps, anything at all. Kit the children can use at camp and take home means golf doesn't end when the camp does.
  2. Send someone to run a golf taster. One person, one visit to Litton Lakes between 17 and 28 August, 60 to 90 minutes getting the whole camp swinging a club for the first time. We handle everything else.
  3. Both, plus what comes next. If it goes well, let's talk about how MDC and the DP World Tour could keep introducing children to golf through our school sessions and future camps.

What the DP World Tour gets

  • A genuine grassroots story in the UK: real children picking up a club for the first time at a real summer camp, alongside Paralympians and other role models.
  • A natural fit with the Tour's commitment to growing the game from the grassroots up.
  • Photos, case studies and parent feedback for your community and social channels.
  • A low-effort way to test working with an established, safeguarding-aware delivery partner. One box of kit or one visit, everything organised.

Next step

The camp runs 17 to 28 August, so kit would need to arrive, or a visit be booked, by early August. A 30-minute call is all it takes to get started. We'll bring the camp plan; you tell us what's realistic from your side.
