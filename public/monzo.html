The Mindful Digital Collective × Monzo

An invitation to join MDC Camps this August, and start money conversations early.

Hey, I'm Nick. My brother-in-law Patrick works at Monzo, and he also helps me run the camp. So I thought it was worth a shot: could we create a small but meaningful partnership, one that benefits hundreds of children now and in the future? Here's the idea.

Who we are

The Mindful Digital Collective is a Community Interest Company delivering digital literacy and life skills workshops in primary schools across Dorset, Surrey and beyond. We run nine established session programmes covering online safety, AI literacy, critical thinking, resilience, and financial literacy. Our aim is simple: confident, thoughtful young people who can navigate the modern world. Money is a huge part of that.

This summer, we're taking that work beyond the classroom with 🏕️ MDC Camps.

The person behind it

MDC was founded by Nick Peasant, a product designer with 20 years in the tech industry. It started in early 2025, when his Year 6 daughter asked for a phone, and he realised that even after a career spent building technology, he didn't know the right answer. That uncertainty became an after-school club at her school, talking with children about life online.

The sessions were never lectures. They're built around real-life scenarios and open discussion: What would you do here? What feels right? How might someone else feel? That approach now spans online safety, AI, scams and fake news, financial literacy, and the value of attention. What Nick has learned from children, parents and teachers is consistent: open conversation builds resilience in children, gives parents confidence, and gives teachers another voice reinforcing what they already teach.

The opportunity: MDC Camps, 17 to 28 August

🏕️ MDC Camps runs at Litton Lakes in Dorset this August. It's two weeks of activity, discussion and inspiration for primary-age children, including funded places through Dorset Council's Holiday Activities and Food programme, so the camp reaches families who need it most.

A core part of the camp is our guest sessions: interesting, inspiring people sharing their stories with the whole camp group. This summer's guests already include Paralympians Taylor Gough and Kylie Grimes MBE, a chef, an LTA tennis coach, and a theatre practitioner. Sessions run 60 to 90 minutes and are discussion-led. The children ask the questions.

We'd love Monzo to be one of those guests. A money session, led by someone from Monzo alongside Nick: what a bank actually does, why saving matters, how to spot a scam. Pitched for children, built on questions and conversation.

Why Monzo

Monzo has already backed this idea harder than any other bank. Monzo for Under 16s opens accounts from age six, with built-in guidance on saving, budgeting and spending safely online. Monzo has also produced classroom learning resources for primary schools in partnership with First News.

Meanwhile, the Money and Pensions Service found that fewer than half of UK children receive a meaningful financial education. Monzo has the tools and a brand these children's families already use. We have the children: outdoors, engaged, and ready to talk.

What we're asking

In order of effort. Any of these would be brilliant:

  1. A Monzo guest at camp. One person, one visit to Litton Lakes between 17 and 28 August, 60 to 90 minutes with the group. We handle everything else.
  2. Something for the children to take home. Monzo-branded materials such as savings goal cards, activity sheets or stickers that carry the message back to families.
  3. Both, plus what comes next. If the camp session goes well, we'd love to talk about Monzo supporting our financial literacy sessions in schools from September.

  • A genuine, on-the-ground story: real children at a real summer camp, alongside Paralympians and other role models, not a marketing campaign.
  • Natural alignment with Monzo for Under 16s and the families who use it.
  • Photos, case studies and parent and teacher feedback for Monzo's community and social channels.
  • A low-effort way to test working with an established, safeguarding-aware delivery partner. One visit, everything organised.
